\begin{abstract}
Two extensions of the TopoSpine pipelines, both with a same-budget baseline and
both willing to report a loss.  On labelled point clouds --- synthetic blobs,
24 MiniLM sentence embeddings, and 12 frames of the MD17 ethanol trajectory ---
a relative cover that is forced to resolve a designated label cuts the
covering radius of that label and, on these examples, \emph{lowers} the chance
that a point and its nearest labelled neighbour fall in the same piece.
On chain complexes, the nullity of the combinatorial Hodge Laplacian reproduces
the $\mathbb F_2$ Betti numbers of the 7-vertex torus and the tetrahedron
boundary, and drops the torsion summand of $\mathbb{R}P^2$.  An elementary
collapse that deletes a free face together with its coface preserves that
nullity while shrinking the chain space; the spectral gap moves; deleting an
arbitrary edge does not preserve the nullity.  No quantum device is used.
GUDHI~3.13 supplies an independent Rips check and an edge-collapse baseline
that does not rewrite the complex.
\end{abstract}

\section{Embedding spines}
The relative tower of Paper~C is applied with $A$ a label class.  Pieces are
balls.  At equal cardinality the comparators are farthest-point $k$-center,
uniform random landmarks, and the coverage-only spine.  Nearest-neighbour
recall is the fraction of points of $A$ whose nearest other point of $A$ is
assigned to the same centre.  A cover that selects every point of $A$ has
radius 0 and recall 0.

\input{../../benchmarks/results/embedding_ablation_table.tex}

On the synthetic blobs and on the 24 MiniLM sentences the relative radius at
the coarsest scale is 0, against about $0.40$ (blobs) and $1.22$ (MiniLM) for
$k$-center at the same number of centres, and the relative recall is 0 against
$0.83$ and $1$.  The forced net is buying resolution by splitting $A$.
Twelve frames of MD17 ethanol (9 atoms, $A$ the four atoms farthest from the
centroid of frame 0; file \texttt{data/md17\_ethanol.npz}, 555\,092 frames of
which 12 are used) gives relative radii $0.34/0.15/0$ against $k$-center
$0.68/0.53/0.34$, and recalls $0.29/0.08/0$ against $0.56/0.46/0.25$.
Same trade.  GUDHI's Rips Betti numbers over $\mathbb F_2$ matched the
in-house routine on every level of these three clouds.  The full ethanol
trajectory was not built into a tower.

\section{Hodge kernels and encoding size}
Let $B_k$ be the real boundary matrix.  The Hodge Laplacian
$L_k=B_k^\top B_k+B_{k+1}B_{k+1}^\top$ has nullity equal to the real Betti
number.  An elementary collapse removes one free simplex and the unique
coface that contains it, and leaves every other face in place.

\input{../../benchmarks/results/quantum_bench_table.tex}

One Boolean square has 9 cells.  The pair collapse leaves 7.  Both have real
Betti numbers $(1,0)$, padded with a zero in degree 2 after the collapse.
The smallest positive eigenvalue of $L_0$ moves from 2 to about $0.586$.
A one-qubit-per-cell encoding of the chain space therefore uses 9 qubits
before and 7 after; the Dirac operator, as an operator on that space, has
those orders.  A $\lceil\log_2\rceil$ encoding of the cell index uses 4 bits
before and 3 after.
Deleting one edge of the 6-cycle that is the $k=3$ suspension changes real
Betti numbers from $(1,1)$ to $(1,0)$.  The stock deletion pass that drops a
whole top cell is a different operation and is not claimed to preserve Betti
numbers.

\section{Collapse-branch filters and one shared netlist}
Paper~A records that cover-admissibility does not imply the transport
hypothesis $(H)$.  On 4\,000 further $n=5$ ON-sets, requiring the free face to
cover the same rows as its parent never fired, nor did restricting to vertex
faces or to parents that are edges.  Requiring the face to cover at most one
row still fired and still disagreed with branch-and-bound.  None of these
filters is a usable sufficient condition on minterm tables.

On ISCAS c17 the per-output pipeline sums to 8 AND gates.  Placing the two
factored covers into one structurally hashed AIG uses 6, which equals both
the NAND2 reference and ABC run on the original netlist.  This is one
circuit.  The hash shares whatever the algebraic factorisation happened to
repeat; it is not a multi-level synthesis proof.
