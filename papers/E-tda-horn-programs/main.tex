\begin{abstract}
Paper~A established Horn retraction (H1), cylinder/cone-prism expansions (H2),
complement-boundary duality (H3), controlled collapsible chains (H4), and
Espresso-Horn escape from \texttt{sccc} stalls with LP/MIS windows (E1).
Paper~B historically reported that \emph{single-face} covering-mode synthesis was
vacuous on a solid $8^3$ voxel blob and on the Coppola--Weiss 2007 cyclic core.
We show Horn theorems are good for TDA \emph{here and now}:
\textsf{HornRetractionCollapsePass} performs $132$ non-vacuous covering steps on
the $8^3$ blob (reducing dimension-$3$ tops $81\to 27$) and $9$ steps on the 2007
core while preserving $H^*(K;\mathbb{F}_2)$.  Spine towers expose certified windows
$L=\max(L_{\mathrm{MIS}},\lceil z_{\mathrm{LP}}\rceil)\le\mathrm{opt}\le U$.
For large two-level instances we wrap classic Berkeley Espresso and an
\textsf{AbcHornSopRefine} SOP pass; full AIG simple-homotopy rewriting is stated
as Conjecture~E1 (explicitly not claimed).
\end{abstract}

\noindent\textbf{Keywords:} topological data analysis, Horn retraction, covering
collapse, Espresso, ABC, certificate-carrying synthesis.

\section{Introduction}
\label{sec:intro}

TopoSpine is a certificate-carrying rewrite engine for cubical complexes and
covering IRs~\cite{paperB}.  Its covering mode previously stalled wherever
single-face admissibility failed---exactly the setting where Horn retraction
$\Lambda_f(f_{\mathrm{top}})=\partial f_{\mathrm{top}}\setminus\{f\}$
(Paper~A, H1) is designed to act.  This note wires H1--H4 and Espresso-Horn
into live programs and measures TDA-relevant deltas: non-vacuous covering
reduction, filtration-compatible compression, and spine cost windows.

\paragraph{Contributions.}
(1)~Non-vacuous Horn covering on medical voxels and the 2007 core (C56).
(2)~Filtration-compatible Horn pass and SpineTower $(L,U)$ windows (C57).
(3)~Classic Espresso CLI adapter and ABC-Horn SOP refine, with AIG Morse left
conjectural (C58).

\section{Program architecture}
\label{sec:arch}

\textsf{PassManager.covering\_horn()} runs only
\textsf{HornRetractionCollapsePass} (GridCube-fast path via
\texttt{collapsible\_cells}).  Homotopy mode prefers
\textsf{FiltrationCompatibleHornPass} before elementary filtration collapse.
\textsf{spine\_min(..., method="horn")} and \textsf{build\_cover\_tower(..., spine\_method="horn")}
attach $(L,U)$ and \texttt{certified\_optimal} to each level.

\section{Conjecture: AIG simple-homotopy}
\label{sec:conjecture}

\begin{conjecture}[E1 --- AIG Forman pairing]
\label{conj:e1}
There exists a discrete Morse pairing on a cubical (or cellular) lift of an ABC
AIG cone that realizes elementary collapses preserving the Boolean function on
the care set and weakly decreasing AND count, extending the SOP Horn refine
already implemented.
\end{conjecture}

\noindent We explicitly \emph{do not} claim Conjecture~\ref{conj:e1} for general
multi-level AIGs until such pairings exist; the SOP refine is the measured cut.

\section{Conclusion}
Horn retraction closes Paper~B's covering vacuity and supplies certified spine
windows.  Classic Espresso plus ABC-Horn SOP refine connect the same theorems to
large two-level practice; AIG Morse remains future work.
